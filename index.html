How to order

Order in four steps

You do not need an account. Tell us what you need and we will take care of the rest.

  1. Pick your products

    Look through the list above and note the product names and how many you need.

  2. Message us

    Tap Order on WhatsApp on any product page and the message is already written for you. You can also call us during opening hours.

  3. We confirm

    We reply with stock, price and how you can pay.

  4. Receive your order

    Collect it from us in Kampala, or ask for delivery around Kampala. Clinics and shops can ask about bulk supply.
